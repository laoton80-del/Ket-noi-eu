# 7. BUSINESS — AI RECEPTIONIST CANONICAL CONTRACT

## 7.1 Ownership

> **AI Receptionist is a Business / B2B capability.**

Business owns:

- merchant setup;
- AI receptionist configuration;
- business hours;
- services;
- staff;
- duration;
- prices supplied by the merchant/system of record;
- availability;
- booking policy;
- table/seat rules where applicable;
- escalation to a human;
- call history;
- analytics;
- customer relationship context;
- operating controls.

## 7.2 Local relationship

Local may expose customer-facing discovery and booking entry points, for example:

- find a Vietnamese nail salon;
- find a restaurant;
- view services;
- request a booking;
- call a business;
- enter a receptionist flow.

But the B2B AI receptionist and merchant-side operational control belong to **Business**.

A clean mental model:

- **Local:** “I need a local service.”
- **Business:** “I provide a service and want VIONA/AI to help me operate.”
- **Alfred/AI:** “I understand intent and execute only permitted actions between the two.”

## 7.3 Target completed experience

Example — nail salon:

1. Customer calls the business number.
2. VIONA AI Receptionist answers when the business has enabled it.
3. AI speaks naturally in the supported language.
4. AI identifies service, preferred time, number of guests, employee preference and constraints.
5. AI checks real merchant availability through an approved tool.
6. AI offers valid alternatives if required.
7. AI creates or requests the booking only within policy.
8. AI confirms the result to the customer.
9. VIONA sends the permitted confirmation/notification.
10. Merchant receives the booking and conversation summary.
11. AI transfers/escalates to a human when necessary.

Example — restaurant:

1. Customer asks for a table.
2. AI determines date, time, party size and preferences.
3. AI checks actual availability/capacity.
4. AI confirms or offers alternatives.
5. AI creates the reservation only through an authorized booking tool.
6. Customer and restaurant receive confirmation.

## 7.4 Calling and autonomous action gate

as appropriate.

Production AI Receptionist requires, at minimum:

- consent/legal readiness;

- permission checks;
- call routing;
- cost controls;
- provider-failure handling;
- human takeover;

Never fake a call, booking, price, table, employee slot, payment, or confirmation.
